Internal Architecture

  • Processors (CPU) The brain of the system. Multi-core architectures and integrated logic.
  • Graphics (GPU) Dedicated and integrated units for parallel processing and visual rendering.
  • System Memory (RAM) DDR4/DDR5 and ECC memory for temporary data handling.
  • Storage Media NVMe/SATA SSDs for speed, and HDDs for high-capacity magnetic storage.
  • The Motherboard The central PCB holding the Chipset, BIOS/UEFI firmware, and expansion slots (PCIe).
  • Power Supply (PSU) Modular and high-efficiency electrical converters (80 Plus ratings).
  • Thermal Management Air coolers, AIO liquid systems, and passive heat sinks.
